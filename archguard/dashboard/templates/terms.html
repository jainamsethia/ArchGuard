Terms

ArchGuard measures things about public code and reports what it measured. It does not promise the measurements are the right ones for your project.

What you may analyse

Public repositories on GitHub. Do not use ArchGuard against code you do not have the right to read, and do not use it to circumvent anyone's access controls.

What the scores mean

A health score is a summary of specific measurements against specific thresholds. It is a starting point for a conversation about a codebase, not a verdict on it, and a low score does not mean the code is bad — often it means the thresholds do not fit the project. Findings are reported with the numbers behind them so you can judge that for yourself.

Availability

Provided as-is, with no guarantee of availability, and analyses may be rate limited or refused. Repositories above a size limit are rejected rather than queued.

Liability

ArchGuard is provided without warranty. Decisions you make about your codebase are yours.
