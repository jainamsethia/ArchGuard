New here? What do these terms mean?
Health Score (0–100, higher is better)
The inverse of ArchDebt: Health = (1 − ArchDebt) × 100. 100 means zero detected architectural debt. Grades: A ≥ 90, B ≥ 80, C ≥ 70, D ≥ 60, F < 60.
Health Band (HEALTHY / WATCH / WARN / FAIL)
Mapped from the ArchDebt composite (0.0–1.0, lower is better). FAIL means CI would block the change.
ArchDebt composite (0.0–1.0, lower is better)
A weighted average of the four layer scores. Health Score is its inverse.
Layer 1 — Import Boundaries
Flags forbidden or unapproved cross-module imports (tree-sitter AST). Violations point to an exact file:line.
Layer 2 — Coupling Delta
Flags modules whose fan-out (unique dependencies) exceeds their coupling budget. Module-level — no single line.
Layer 3 — Semantic Drift
Flags modules whose embedding centroid drifted beyond the threshold since the baseline. Requires ML extras.
Layer 4 — Duplication
Flags cross-module function clones found via vector similarity (FAISS). Requires ML extras.
Severity
Critical fails CI immediately; High blocks if enabled; Medium is significant tech debt; Low is a minor warning.
Suppression
A deliberately ignored violation (false positive or accepted debt). Suppressed violations are excluded from the score and the Active Violations count.

⚠ Module boundaries were guessed, not measured
